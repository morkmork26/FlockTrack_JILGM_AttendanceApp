What is FlockTrack?

FlockTrack is a Web App (PWA) built for JILGM Sto. Tomas to track Sunday attendance. It works on any phone or tablet, can be installed like a native app, and works offline. Data syncs to Google Sheets via Google Apps Script.

Setup Guide

Follow these steps to get FlockTrack running on your device.

1

Fill Up the Registration Form

Before anything else, fill up this form so we can register you as a user:
Click here to fill up the registration form

2

Open the App Link

Open the GitHub Pages link in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone):
https://morkmork26.github.io/JILGM-Attendance/

3

Install the App (Recommended)

Android: Tap the "Install App" banner or go to Chrome menu > "Add to Home Screen".
iPhone: Tap the Share button > "Add to Home Screen".
This gives you a full-screen app experience without the browser toolbar.

4

Start Using

Once approved, you land on the Check-In tab. Choose QR Scan, Quick Tap, or Search mode to start checking in members every Sunday.

Note: The app only needs internet to sync data. If you're offline, check-ins are saved locally and will sync automatically when you reconnect.

App Tabs Overview

☑ Check-In Tab

  • QR Scan - Use phone camera to scan member QR codes
  • Quick Tap - Avatar grid; tap a member's face to check them in (green ring = checked in)
  • Search - Type a name to find and check in
  • Counter badge at top right shows checked-in / total
  • Filter by ministry in Quick Tap mode

👥 Members Tab

  • Search and browse all registered members
  • Filter by Active/Inactive status
  • Filter by Ministry or Outreach location
  • Tap a member to view profile, attendance streak, history
  • Use the + button (FAB) to add new members

📊 Reports Tab

  • Stats cards: Checked In Today, Total Members, Children, New This Month, Weekly Average
  • Ministry Breakdown chart
  • Weekly Attendance bar chart (last several Sundays)
  • Needs Follow-Up list (absent 2+ Sundays)
  • Children count input for Sunday School
  • "Generate Report" button to email summary

⚙ Settings Tab

  • Appearance - Dark/Light mode toggle
  • Connection - Google Apps Script URL (backend)
  • Report - Email address, default outreach location
  • Sync - Manual sync, view pending changes
  • Data - Export attendance as CSV
  • Ministries - Add/remove ministry tags
  • Occasions - Add special events (Christmas, Anniversary, etc.)
  • Feedback - Send suggestions or bug reports

FAQ & Tips

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. The app caches everything locally via Service Worker. Check-ins are saved offline and sync when you're back online. The green dot in the header shows sync status.

What if the QR scanner doesn't work?

Make sure you've allowed camera permission. You can also use Quick Tap or Search mode as alternatives. Tap "Flip Camera" to switch between front/back camera.

How do I know someone is checked in?

Their avatar gets a green ring around it, and the counter badge updates (e.g., "15/42"). A success toast notification also appears.

What are "Occasions"?

Special events like Christmas Service, Anniversary, Youth Night, etc. When an occasion is active, a banner appears at the top of Check-In and attendance is tagged with that event name.

How does Follow-Up work?

Members who have missed 2 or more consecutive Sundays appear in the "Needs Follow-Up" section of the Reports tab, so leaders can reach out.
